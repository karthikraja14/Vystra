"How many pages do I need?" is one of the most common questions — and the honest answer is: exactly as many as your customers need, and no more. Here's how to work it out.

## Start with the customer's questions
Every page should answer a question or serve a goal. List what customers ask before they buy — each cluster of questions often becomes a page.

## The typical core pages

- **Home** — who you are, what you do, why choose you.
- **About** — trust and story.
- **Services / Products** — one page each if they're distinct.
- **Contact** — easy ways to reach you.
- **Blog** — if you'll publish content.

## When you need more
- **Multiple distinct services** — a page each helps you rank for each.
- **Location pages** — if you serve several areas.
- **Case studies** — proof for higher-value buyers.

## When fewer is better
A small local business may thrive on a strong **single-page site** or a handful of pages. More pages with nothing to say just dilutes your site.

## Quality over quantity
Ten thin pages hurt you; five strong ones help. Each page should earn its place with a clear purpose and useful content.

Build pages around customer needs and search demand — not a page count. At Vystra we map your pages to how customers actually decide, so every page pulls its weight.
