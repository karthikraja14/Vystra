You've seen them everywhere: "This site uses cookies." That popup is a **cookie banner**, and whether you need one depends on what your website tracks.

## What cookies are
Cookies are small files stored in a visitor's browser. Some are essential (keeping you logged in, remembering a cart); others track behaviour for **analytics or advertising**.

## What the banner is for
A cookie banner tells visitors your site uses cookies and — where required — asks their consent before non-essential tracking runs. It's about transparency and consent.

## Do you actually need one?
- **Using analytics, ads or tracking?** You likely need a consent banner, especially if you have visitors from regions with strict privacy laws.
- **A simple site with only essential cookies?** You may only need a brief notice, or none — but a short mention is still good practice.

India's DPDP Act and global rules are pushing all sites toward clearer consent, so it's wise to handle it properly.

## How to do it right
- **Be clear, not annoying.** A simple, readable banner — not a screen-blocking maze.
- **Offer a real choice** where you use tracking (accept / reject).
- **Don't run non-essential cookies before consent.**
- **Link to your privacy policy** for details.

## A common mistake
Copying a fancy banner that then loads tracking *anyway* before the visitor agrees. If you ask for consent, honour it.
