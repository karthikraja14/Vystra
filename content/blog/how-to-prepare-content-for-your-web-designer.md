The number one reason website projects stall isn't design or code — it's **waiting for content**. If you prepare your content early, your site gets built faster and turns out better. Here's your checklist.

## What your designer needs from you

**1. Your logo and branding.**
Logo files, brand colours if you have them, and any fonts you use.

**2. Text for each page.**
What you do, your services, your story, your contact details. Even rough drafts help — a good designer can polish them.

**3. Images.**
Photos of your work, team and premises. Real photos beat stock every time.

**4. Your services and pricing.**
A clear list of what you offer, with descriptions.

**5. Customer reviews or testimonials.**
Real quotes from happy customers build trust.

**6. Contact details.**
Phone, email, address, WhatsApp, social links, business hours.

## Things that speed everything up
- **List your pages.** Home, About, Services, Contact — decide the structure early.
- **Note examples you like.** Share a few websites you admire and why.
- **Define your goal.** More calls? More sales? It shapes the whole design.

## Don't worry about perfection
You don't need polished, final copy. Rough content is enough to start — it's far better than waiting weeks for "perfect" text. Your designer can refine it.

## The takeaway
Ready content is the secret to a fast, smooth website project. Gather your logo, text, images, services and reviews upfront. At Vystra we guide you through exactly what's needed — so your site launches quickly and looks great.
