Most websites are built to *look* good, not to *convert*. A site can have traffic and still generate zero enquiries. Here's how to fix that.

## Lead with the outcome, not yourself
Visitors care about their problem, not your company history. Your headline should state the result you deliver in one line.

## Make the next step obvious
Every page should have one clear call to action — "Book a call", "Get a quote". If visitors have to hunt for how to contact you, they leave.

## Show proof
Reviews, results, client logos and before/after examples do more than any adjective. Specific numbers ("+180% leads") beat vague claims.

## Reduce friction
- Short forms — ask only what you need.
- A visible WhatsApp or call button for mobile users.
- Fast load, clean on phones.

## Answer objections
Price, trust and risk stop people from acting. Address them directly with FAQs, guarantees or transparent pricing.

## Match the message to the visitor
Someone arriving from a "wedding photographer Coimbatore" search should land on a page about exactly that — not a generic homepage.

## The takeaway
Conversion is design plus psychology: clear promise, strong proof, one obvious action, minimal friction. At Vystra we design every page around the single action that grows your revenue — so traffic turns into clients.
