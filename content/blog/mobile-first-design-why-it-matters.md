In India, the majority of web traffic is on mobile — often 70% or more. If your site is designed for desktop first, most of your visitors get the worst version. Here's why mobile-first wins.

## What "mobile-first" means
It means designing for the **phone screen first**, then scaling up to desktop — not shrinking a desktop site to fit a phone. The priorities, layout and tap targets are built for small screens from the start.

## Why it matters

- **Google uses mobile-first indexing** — it ranks your site based on the mobile version.
- **Most visitors are on phones** — a clumsy mobile experience loses the majority.
- **Conversions happen on mobile** — slow or fiddly checkouts and forms cost sales.

## What a mobile-ready site needs

1. **Fast load** on mobile data.
2. **Readable text** without zooming.
3. **Big, tappable buttons** — no tiny links.
4. **Thumb-friendly** navigation.
5. **Forms that are easy** on a phone keyboard.
6. **Visible contact** — click-to-call, WhatsApp.

## Common mobile failures
Tiny fonts, pop-ups that can't be closed, horizontal scrolling, and buttons too close together. Each one quietly sends visitors away.

## The takeaway
Mobile isn't a version of your site — for most businesses it *is* your site. At Vystra every build is mobile-first and fast, because that's where your customers actually are.
