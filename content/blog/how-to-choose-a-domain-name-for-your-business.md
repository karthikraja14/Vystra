Your domain name (yourbusiness.com) is your permanent address on the internet. You'll say it on calls, print it on cards and use it in email — so it's worth choosing well.

## What makes a good domain

**1. Short and simple.**
Easier to remember, type and say aloud. Avoid long, complicated names.

**2. Easy to spell.**
If people can't spell it after hearing it once, they can't find you. Avoid tricky spellings and silent letters.

**3. Brandable.**
Ideally your business name. If taken, add a small word rather than a random string.

**4. No hyphens or numbers.**
"best-builders-2" is hard to say and looks less trustworthy. Avoid them.

## Which extension? (.com vs .in)
- **.com** — most recognised and trusted worldwide.
- **.in** — good for India-focused businesses; often available when .com isn't.

For most Indian small businesses, .com or .in both work well. Grab both if you can, and point one to the other.

## Quick tips
- Say it out loud — does it sound clear on a phone call?
- Check social media handles are free too.
- Avoid trademarks you don't own.
- Register for multiple years to protect it.
