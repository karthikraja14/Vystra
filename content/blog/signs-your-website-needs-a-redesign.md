A bad website doesn't announce itself. It just quietly turns visitors away while you wonder why enquiries are slow. Here are eight signs yours needs a rebuild.

## 1. It loads slowly

If your site takes more than 2–3 seconds on mobile, a large share of visitors leave before it even appears. Speed is both a conversion factor and a Google ranking factor.

## 2. It looks dated

Design signals trust. If your site looks like it's from 2015, visitors assume the business is behind the times too — fair or not.

## 3. It's hard to use on a phone

More than half your traffic is mobile. Tiny text, broken layouts or hard-to-tap buttons lose the majority of your audience instantly.

## 4. The message is unclear

If a visitor can't tell what you do and what to do next within a few seconds, they leave. Clarity beats cleverness.

## 5. There's no obvious next step

Every page needs one clear call to action. If yours buries the "contact" or "buy" button, you're leaving money on the table.

## 6. It doesn't show up on Google

If you can't find your site by searching your own services, neither can customers. That's an SEO and structure problem worth fixing.

## 7. You can't update it yourself

If every small text change needs a developer, your site is a liability, not an asset.

## 8. Enquiries have gone quiet

The clearest sign of all: traffic exists but leads don't. Something between "visit" and "contact" is broken.

## What to do

You don't always need to start from scratch. A good redesign audits what you have, keeps what works, and rebuilds the rest faster, cleaner and more persuasive.
