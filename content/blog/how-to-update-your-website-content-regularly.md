A website isn't a "build it and forget it" project. A site that never changes slowly loses ground — visitors sense it's outdated, and Google tends to favour sites that stay active. Keeping content fresh pays off.

## Why fresh content matters
- **Trust** — old prices, outdated info or "© 2019" signal neglect.
- **SEO** — Google likes sites that are maintained and updated.
- **Relevance** — your services, offers and photos should reflect *now*.
- **Repeat visits** — new content gives people a reason to return.

## What "fresh" actually means
It doesn't mean redesigning constantly. It means:
- Keeping **prices and info accurate**.
- Adding **recent work** or photos.
- Refreshing **reviews** and testimonials.
- Publishing the occasional **blog post** or update.
- Updating the **year** and any dated references.

## Simple ways to keep it fresh
1. **Add new work** as you complete projects.
2. **Post occasionally** — even one useful article a month helps.
3. **Update your offers** seasonally.
4. **Review yearly** — check every page for outdated info.

## The easiest win: a simple blog
Even a light blog (like this one) gives Google new pages to index and gives customers helpful, trust-building content — without redesigning anything.
