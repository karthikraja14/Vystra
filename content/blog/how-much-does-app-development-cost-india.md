"How much for an app?" is like asking the price of a vehicle — a scooter and a truck are both "vehicles". Here's a realistic 2026 guide for India.

## What drives the cost

- **Complexity** — a simple app vs one with payments, chat, real-time data.
- **Platforms** — Android only, or Android + iOS.
- **Backend** — logins, database, admin panel, APIs.
- **Design** — template vs custom.

## Rough 2026 ranges

| App type | Indicative cost |
| --- | --- |
| Simple (info, forms) | ₹1–4 lakh |
| Medium (accounts, payments) | ₹4–12 lakh |
| Complex (marketplace, real-time) | ₹12 lakh+ |

These are indicative — scope decides the real number.

## Hidden costs to plan for
- **Backend hosting** and ongoing running costs.
- **App store fees** and publishing.
- **Maintenance and updates** — apps need upkeep as phones change.

## Save money without cutting quality
- **Start with an MVP** — build the core, validate, then expand.
- **Cross-platform** frameworks can cover Android + iOS from one codebase.
- **Nail the scope** before building — changes mid-project are the most expensive.
