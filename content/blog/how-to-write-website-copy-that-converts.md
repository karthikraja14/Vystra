Design gets people to stop. Words get them to act. Most small-business websites look fine but say the wrong things — so visitors leave.

## Write about them, not you
Replace "We are a leading provider of…" with "You get…". Customers care about their problem, not your company history.

## Lead with the benefit
Don't say "24/7 monitoring." Say "Sleep easy — we watch your site around the clock." Feature → benefit.

## Use their words
Read the questions customers actually ask and use that exact language. If they say "cheap and reliable," don't write "cost-effective solutions."

## Keep it short and scannable
- Short sentences.
- Sub-headings every few lines.
- Bullet points for lists.
- One idea per paragraph.

People skim. Make skimming work.

## Every page needs a next step
End each section with a clear action: "Get a quote," "Book a call," "See pricing." Never leave a reader wondering what to do.

## The takeaway
You don't need clever slogans — you need clear, customer-focused words that answer "what's in it for me?" At Vystra we pair strong design with copy that guides visitors toward getting in touch.
