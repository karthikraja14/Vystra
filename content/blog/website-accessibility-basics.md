Accessibility means building your site so everyone can use it — including people with visual, hearing or motor differences. It's the right thing to do, and it quietly helps your SEO and conversions too.

## Why it matters

- **More people can use your site** — a real slice of every audience.
- **Better SEO** — many accessibility practices (clear structure, alt text) help Google too.
- **Better for everyone** — clear, readable design helps all visitors.

## Simple things to get right

1. **Alt text on images** — describe what the image shows.
2. **Good colour contrast** — text must be readable against its background.
3. **Readable font sizes** — no tiny text.
4. **Keyboard navigation** — the site should work without a mouse.
5. **Clear headings** — proper structure, not just big bold text.
6. **Labels on form fields** — so screen readers can guide users.
7. **Captions/transcripts** for video and audio.

## Common failures
Light grey text on white, images with no alt text, forms with no labels, and buttons that only work with a mouse. Each one shuts someone out.

## The takeaway
Accessibility isn't a separate project — it's just good web design done properly. At Vystra we build with these basics in place, so your site works for every visitor and reads well to search engines.
