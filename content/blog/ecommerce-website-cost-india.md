An online store can cost ₹40,000 or ₹4,00,000 — and both can be "right." The difference is not the agency being greedy; it's what the store actually has to do. Here is how to figure out where your project lands.

## The three cost tiers

**1. Template store (₹40,000 – ₹90,000).** Built on Shopify or WooCommerce with a polished theme, your branding, product upload, payment and shipping setup. Fast to launch, perfect for a first store or up to a few hundred products.

**2. Custom-designed store (₹1,00,000 – ₹2,50,000).** A bespoke design built around *your* brand and buying journey, custom product pages, filters, and conversion tuning. This is where average order value and conversion rate start to climb.

**3. Custom-built platform (₹3,00,000+).** Headless or fully custom builds with complex catalogs, subscriptions, multi-vendor, ERP/inventory integrations or unusual checkout logic. This is software engineering, not theme setup.

## What actually moves the price

- **Number of products and variants** — 20 products is easy; 5,000 with sizes and colours needs real data structure.
- **Integrations** — payment gateways are standard; ERP, GST invoicing, courier APIs and CRMs add work.
- **Design depth** — a theme tweak vs a custom design system.
- **Content** — who writes product descriptions and shoots photos? Good content sells; it also costs time.

## Ongoing costs people forget

- Hosting / platform fees (Shopify plans, or your own hosting).
- Payment gateway transaction fees (~2% typical).
- Maintenance, security updates and occasional design refreshes.

## How to spend smart

Start at the tier your catalog and traffic justify — not the fanciest one. A clean template store that converts beats a custom platform you outgrow slowly. You can always upgrade once revenue proves the demand.
