SEO and SEM both help people find you on Google, but they're not the same thing — and confusing them wastes money. Here's the plain difference.

## SEO — the free (earned) traffic
**Search Engine Optimisation** is about ranking in the *unpaid* search results. You improve your site — content, speed, keywords, structure — so Google shows it naturally.

- **Cost:** mostly time and effort, not per-click.
- **Speed:** slow to build, but lasting.
- **Trust:** people often trust organic results more.

## SEM — the paid traffic
**Search Engine Marketing** (usually Google Ads) is about *paying* to appear at the top of search results, marked "Sponsored."

- **Cost:** you pay per click.
- **Speed:** instant — you can be at the top today.
- **Control:** you choose keywords, budget and audience.

## The simple way to remember it
- **SEO** = earn your spot over time (free clicks, slow).
- **SEM** = buy your spot instantly (paid clicks, fast).

## Which should you start with?
- **Need customers *now*?** SEM/ads give immediate traffic while SEO builds.
- **Building for the long term?** SEO gives compounding, lasting results without paying per click.

Most businesses eventually use both: ads for quick wins, SEO for sustainable growth.
