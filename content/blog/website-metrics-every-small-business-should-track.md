Website analytics can feel overwhelming — dozens of charts and numbers. But for a small business, only a handful of metrics actually matter. Track these and ignore the rest.

## The metrics that matter

**1. Visitors (traffic).**
How many people come to your site, and whether it's growing. The starting point for everything else.

**2. Traffic sources.**
*How* people find you — Google search, social media, direct, or referrals. Tells you what's working so you can do more of it.

**3. Top pages.**
Which pages get the most visits. Shows what people care about most.

**4. Enquiries / conversions.**
The most important one: how many visitors actually **contact you, call, or buy**. Traffic without enquiries isn't paying off.

**5. Bounce rate.**
How many leave without engaging — a clue to problems on key pages.

**6. Device split.**
How many visit on mobile vs desktop (usually mostly mobile — which is why mobile matters so much).

## The one number to focus on
**Enquiries.** A site's job is to bring you business. 10,000 visitors and zero enquiries means something's broken; 200 visitors and 20 enquiries is a success. Always tie traffic back to real results.

## How to track them
Free tools like **Google Analytics** and **Search Console** cover everything above. You don't need anything fancy or paid to start.
