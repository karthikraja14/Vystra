For any business with local customers, a Google Business Profile is the single highest-return free marketing you can set up. Here's how to do it properly.

## Why it matters
When someone searches "web designer near me" or "cafe in Coimbatore", Google shows a map with three highlighted businesses. That's the **Business Profile** at work — and it often gets more clicks than the website results below it.

## Step-by-step setup

1. **Create/claim** your profile at Google Business Profile.
2. **Verify** ownership (usually by postcard, phone or email).
3. **Fill everything** — name, category, address, hours, phone, website.
4. **Add photos** — real ones of your work, team and location.
5. **Choose accurate categories** — the primary category matters most.

## Optimise for ranking

- **Consistent NAP** (name, address, phone) across the web.
- **Collect reviews** — and reply to them.
- **Post updates** — offers, news, photos.
- **Add services/products** with descriptions.

## The review engine
Reviews are the biggest local ranking factor you control. Ask happy customers, make it easy with a direct link, and always respond — it signals an active, trusted business.
