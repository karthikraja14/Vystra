A blog can be one of the best long-term SEO investments — or a graveyard of three posts from two years ago. Here's how to decide if it's right for you.

## What a blog actually does
Each useful article is a new door into your site from Google. Answer the questions your customers search, and you show up when they're looking — for years, without paying per click.

## When a blog is worth it

- Your customers **research before buying**.
- You can answer **real questions** they have.
- You want **long-term, compounding** traffic instead of only ads.
- You're willing to publish **consistently**.

## When it isn't

- You need leads **this week** (ads are faster).
- You **can't commit** to publishing regularly.
- Your business has **almost no search demand**.

## How to do it without wasting time

1. Write for **real search queries**, not company news.
2. Make each post **genuinely useful** — one strong post beats five thin ones.
3. Publish on a **steady schedule**, not in bursts.
4. **Link** posts to your services.
