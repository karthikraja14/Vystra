You can't improve what you can't measure. Google Analytics (free) shows how people find and use your site. Here's what it does and whether it's worth setting up.

## What it tracks

- **How many visitors** you get.
- **Where they come from** — Google, social, direct, ads.
- **What they do** — pages viewed, time on site, where they leave.
- **What converts** — which sources lead to enquiries or sales.

## Why it's useful
It answers the questions that guide decisions: *Is my SEO working? Which page loses people? Are ads paying off?* Without data, you're guessing.

## The numbers that actually matter
Ignore vanity metrics. Focus on:
1. **Traffic sources** — where results come from.
2. **Top pages** — what's working.
3. **Conversions** — enquiries, calls, sales.
4. **Drop-off points** — where visitors leave.

## Do you need it?
- **Yes**, if you want to grow deliberately and spend on marketing wisely.
- It's **free** and low-effort to add.
- Pair it with **Google Search Console** to see what you rank for.

## A note on privacy
Be transparent — mention analytics in your privacy policy, as expected under data norms.
