Which business would you trust more: one that emails from `rajeshbuilders@gmail.com`, or one from `rajesh@rajeshbuilders.com`? A business email on your own domain instantly looks more established.

## Why it matters
- **Trust** — a branded email signals a real, professional business.
- **Free marketing** — every email reinforces your domain name.
- **Consistency** — your website, email and brand all match.
- **Control** — you own it, not tied to one person's personal account.

## What you need
1. **A domain** — the same one as your website (yourbusiness.com).
2. **An email hosting service** — such as Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 (or basic email included with some hosting).

Once set up, you get addresses like info@, sales@ or your-name@yourbusiness.com.

## A few good addresses to create
- **info@** or **hello@** — general enquiries.
- **your-name@** — personal/direct.
- **sales@** or **support@** — as you grow.
