SEO has a reputation for being complicated and slow. Most of that is noise. For a small business, the fundamentals are simple — and doing them well beats 90% of competitors who don't bother.

## What SEO actually is

Search engines try to show the most useful, trustworthy page for each search. SEO is just making your page the obvious best answer — and making it easy for Google to read.

## The five things that actually matter

1. **Target real search phrases.** Write pages around what people type — "GST billing software for small business", not "solutions for enterprises".
2. **Fast, mobile-first site.** Most Indian traffic is mobile. A slow site loses rankings and customers.
3. **Genuinely useful content.** One strong page that answers a question beats ten thin ones.
4. **Local signals.** For local businesses, a Google Business Profile and consistent name/address/phone are huge.
5. **Other sites linking to you.** Backlinks from real, relevant sites still carry weight.

## What to ignore

- Keyword stuffing — it hurts now.
- Buying cheap backlinks — risky and often penalised.
- Chasing every algorithm rumour.

## Where to start on a small budget

Pick five phrases your customers actually search, build one clear page for each, make the site fast on mobile, and set up your Google Business Profile. That foundation compounds for years.
