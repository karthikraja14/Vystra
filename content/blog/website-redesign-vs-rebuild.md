When a website feels tired or underperforms, there are two paths: refresh what's there, or start again. Choosing wrong wastes money either way. Here's how to decide.

## Redesign — refresh the existing site
Keep the underlying site and update the look, layout and content.

**Best when:**
- The foundation is sound and reasonably fast.
- It's mainly the *look* that's dated.
- Structure and content are mostly working.

## Rebuild — start from scratch
Build a new site on new foundations.

**Best when:**
- The site is **slow and bloated** with no easy fix.
- It's **not mobile-friendly**.
- The platform is outdated or insecure.
- You **don't own or can't edit** it.
- Business goals have fundamentally changed.

## The honest test
Ask: *are the problems skin-deep or structural?* Skin-deep → redesign. Structural (speed, mobile, security, ownership) → rebuild. Redesigning a broken foundation just puts fresh paint on a cracked wall.

## Don't lose your SEO
Whichever you choose, preserve your URLs and content value, and redirect old pages properly — otherwise you can lose hard-won rankings overnight.
