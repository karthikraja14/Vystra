Before people call you, they read your reviews. Online reviews are today's word of mouth — and they quietly decide whether a stranger trusts your business or scrolls to a competitor.

## Why reviews matter so much
- **Trust** — people believe other customers more than your own claims.
- **Decisions** — most buyers read reviews before choosing.
- **Local search** — Google ranks businesses with more (and better) reviews higher on Maps.
- **Feedback** — they tell you what's working and what isn't.

## Where reviews count most
- **Google Business Profile** — the big one for local search.
- **Facebook** and industry-specific platforms.
- **Your own website** — showcasing reviews builds instant credibility.

## How to get more reviews
1. **Just ask.** Most happy customers will leave one if asked directly.
2. **Make it easy.** Send a direct link to your Google review page.
3. **Ask at the right moment** — right after a good result.
4. **Follow up** with a polite reminder.

## Handling negative reviews
Don't panic or argue. Respond calmly, apologise if needed, and offer to fix it. A professional reply to a bad review often impresses future customers *more* than a perfect record.

## Show them off
Display your best reviews on your website — near your services and contact form, where they help close the deal.
