For most brands, the e-commerce question comes down to Shopify versus a custom-built store. Both are good choices — for different situations. Here's how to decide without regret.

## Where Shopify wins

- **Speed to launch.** You can be selling in days, not weeks.
- **Low maintenance.** Hosting, security and updates are handled for you.
- **Ecosystem.** Thousands of apps for reviews, shipping, subscriptions and more.
- **Reliability.** It handles payments, traffic spikes and PCI compliance out of the box.

**Shopify is ideal if:** you want to launch fast, sell a manageable catalog, and not worry about infrastructure.

## Where a custom store wins

- **Full control.** Any design, any checkout flow, any logic you can imagine.
- **No platform limits.** You're not boxed in by what a theme or app allows.
- **Unique experience.** For brands where the buying experience *is* the differentiator.
- **Deep integrations.** Custom ERP, inventory or B2B pricing that platforms struggle with.

**Custom is ideal if:** you have unusual requirements, a large or complex catalog, or the buying experience is central to your brand.

## The honest middle path

Most growing brands should **start on Shopify** and go custom only when a real limitation forces it. Premature custom builds cost more and slow you down before you've proven demand. Let revenue justify the upgrade.

## The deciding question

Ask: *is Shopify actually stopping me from doing something important?* If no, stay and grow. If yes, and it's costing you sales, that's when custom pays off.
