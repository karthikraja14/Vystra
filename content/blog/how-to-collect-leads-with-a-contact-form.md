Your contact form is the moment a visitor becomes a lead. Yet many businesses lose enquiries here — the form is too long, confusing, or quietly broken. A good form captures every interested visitor.

## Keep it short
Every extra field loses people. Ask only for what you truly need:
- **Name**
- **Phone or email** (one is often enough)
- **Message**

You can gather the rest during the conversation. Long forms with ten fields scare people off.

## Make it obvious what happens next
Tell them: "We'll get back to you within 24 hours." Uncertainty causes hesitation.

## Add a WhatsApp alternative
Many people won't fill a form but *will* send a WhatsApp message. Offer both — you'll capture more leads.

## Test it regularly
The most expensive mistake in business websites: a contact form that silently stopped sending. Test yours often and make sure submissions actually reach your inbox.

## Confirm the submission
After someone submits, show a clear "Thanks — we've received your message." Without it, people wonder if it worked and resubmit or leave.
