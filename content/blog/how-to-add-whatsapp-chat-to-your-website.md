In India, people are far more comfortable sending a WhatsApp message than filling out a form or making a call. Putting a WhatsApp button on your website removes friction — and more messages means more business.

## Why WhatsApp beats a contact form
- **Familiar** — everyone already uses it.
- **Instant** — no waiting for an email reply.
- **Low effort** — one tap, no typing name/email/phone.
- **Trust** — a real conversation feels personal.

## How it works
A WhatsApp chat button is a floating icon on your site. When tapped, it opens WhatsApp with your number and a pre-filled message like *"Hi, I saw your website and I'm interested in…"* — so the customer just hits send.

## Where to place it
- A floating button in the bottom corner (visible on every page).
- Near your main call-to-action.
- On your contact page.

## A tip that helps
Pre-fill a message so the customer doesn't have to think. Reducing that tiny bit of effort noticeably increases how many people actually message you.
