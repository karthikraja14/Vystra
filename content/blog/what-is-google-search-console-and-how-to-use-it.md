Google Search Console is a free tool from Google that shows how your website appears in search — what people search to find you, which pages rank, and any problems Google sees. Every business with a website should use it.

## What it tells you

**1. What people search to find you.**
The actual search terms bringing visitors — gold for understanding your customers.

**2. Which pages rank and how well.**
See your positions, clicks and impressions per page.

**3. Indexing status.**
Whether Google has indexed your pages, and any that failed.

**4. Problems to fix.**
Mobile usability issues, security problems, and errors that hurt rankings.

## How to set it up
1. Go to [Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Add your site and verify ownership (usually a DNS record or a file).
3. Submit your sitemap in the *Sitemaps* report.

## How to actually use it (monthly)
- Check the **Performance** report — what's growing, what's slipping.
- See which **search terms** you rank for, and create content around them.
- Fix anything flagged in the **Indexing** or **Experience** reports.
- Submit new important pages for indexing.

## One important tip
Don't paste sitemap or page URLs into the wrong place. Sitemaps go in the **Sitemaps** report; individual pages go in **URL Inspection**. Mixing these up causes confusing "errors."
