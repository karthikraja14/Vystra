If you sell anything, letting customers pay on your site removes friction and captures sales you'd otherwise lose. Here's how it works in India.

## What you need
- A **payment gateway** (the service that processes cards, UPI, netbanking).
- A **way to trigger payment** on your site — a checkout, a payment link, or a form.
- A **bank account** for settlements.

## Popular options in India
Gateways like Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree and others support **UPI, cards, netbanking and wallets**. UPI is essential — it's how most Indian customers prefer to pay.

## What it costs
Gateways typically charge a **small percentage per transaction** (often around 2%). There's usually no big upfront cost for a basic setup.

## Simple vs full ecommerce
- **Payment links / a single product** — quick, minimal build.
- **Full store** — catalogue, cart, inventory, order management.

Match the setup to how much you sell. Don't build a full store for one product.

## Don't forget
- **Security** — use the gateway's hosted checkout; never handle raw card data yourself.
- **Clear pricing and refund policy** — builds trust and reduces disputes.
- **Mobile checkout** — most payments happen on phones.
