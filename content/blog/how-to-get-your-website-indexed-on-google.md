You built a website but it doesn't show up when you Google it. That's normal at first — Google has to *index* your site before it can appear in search. Here's how to speed that up.

## What "indexing" means
Google sends crawlers to discover pages, then stores them in its index. Only indexed pages can appear in search results. A brand-new site simply hasn't been indexed yet.

## Steps to get indexed faster

**1. Set up Google Search Console.**
Verify your site in [Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console). This is the official tool to talk to Google about your site — and it's free.

**2. Submit your sitemap.**
In Search Console's *Sitemaps* report, submit yoursite.com/sitemap.xml. This hands Google a list of all your pages.

**3. Use "Request Indexing."**
Use the URL Inspection tool on your key pages and click *Request Indexing* to nudge Google.

**4. Get a few links.**
Links from other sites (your social profiles, Google Business Profile, directories) help Google discover you.

## How long does it take?
Anywhere from a few days to a few weeks for a new site. Be patient — repeatedly resubmitting doesn't speed it up.

## A common mistake
Blocking Google by accident — a stray "noindex" setting or a robots.txt rule can keep your site invisible. Search Console will warn you if this happens.
