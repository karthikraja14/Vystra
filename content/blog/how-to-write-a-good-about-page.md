The About page is one of the most visited pages on almost every website — people go there to decide if they can trust you. Yet most About pages are dull, vague, and all about the company. Here's how to do it right.

## It's not really about you
Counterintuitive, but true: a great About page connects *your* story to *their* problem. Visitors are asking "can these people help me, and can I trust them?" Answer that.

## What to include

**1. What you do and who you help.**
In plain words, right at the top. Don't make people guess.

**2. Your story — briefly.**
Why you started, what you believe. A little personality builds connection. Keep it short.

**3. Proof you're credible.**
Experience, results, number of clients, reviews, certifications.

**4. The faces behind the business.**
Real photos of real people. This alone dramatically increases trust for small businesses.

**5. A call to action.**
End with a next step — "Get in touch," "See our work."

## What to avoid
- Corporate jargon ("synergistic solutions provider").
- A wall of text with no structure.
- Making it *only* about your history with no benefit to the reader.
- Stock photos of strangers in suits.

## Keep it human
Write like you'd talk to a customer. Warmth and honesty beat polished corporate language every time.

A strong About page turns curiosity into trust. Show who you are, who you help, and why you're credible — then invite them to act. At Vystra we help craft About pages that make small businesses feel real and trustworthy.
