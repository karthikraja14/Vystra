If your website collects *any* information from visitors — even just a contact form or Google Analytics — you should have a privacy policy. It's part legal requirement, part trust signal.

## Why you need one
- **It's often required.** Data protection laws (and India's DPDP Act) expect you to tell people how you use their data.
- **Third parties require it.** Google Analytics, ad platforms and payment providers expect a privacy policy.
- **It builds trust.** Visitors are more comfortable sharing details when you're transparent.

## When you need one
Basically whenever your site:
- Has a contact or enquiry form.
- Uses analytics or cookies.
- Runs ads or retargeting.
- Takes payments.
- Collects emails for a newsletter.

That covers almost every business website.

## What it should cover
- What information you collect (name, email, phone).
- How you use it.
- Who you share it with (e.g. analytics, payment providers).
- How people can contact you about their data.

## Keep it honest and simple
Don't copy a random policy that doesn't match your site. It should truthfully reflect what *you* actually collect and do.
