If you have ever asked three agencies for a website quote and received prices ranging from ₹5,000 to ₹5,00,000, you are not alone. Website pricing in India is confusing on purpose. This guide fixes that.

## What actually drives website cost

The price of a website comes down to four things:

1. **Scope** — how many pages, and how custom the design is.
2. **Functionality** — a brochure site is cheap; a booking system, dashboard or payment flow is not.
3. **Design quality** — templates are fast and cheap; bespoke design that converts takes real work.
4. **Who builds it** — a freelancer, an agency, or a product studio.

## Realistic 2026 price bands

- **Landing page (1 page):** ₹12,000 – ₹25,000. Perfect for validating an offer or running ads.
- **Business website (4–8 pages):** ₹30,000 – ₹80,000. Includes a design system, CMS and proper SEO.
- **E-commerce store:** ₹60,000 – ₹2,00,000+ depending on catalog size and integrations.
- **Web app / SaaS MVP:** ₹1,50,000+ and up. This is software, not a website.

## Red flags that you are overpaying

- Monthly "rental" websites you never own.
- Vague quotes with no fixed deliverables or timeline.
- Being charged separately for basic SEO, mobile responsiveness or an SSL certificate — these should be standard in 2026.
