Most small business websites don't fail dramatically — they just quietly underperform because of a handful of avoidable mistakes. Here's the checklist.

## 1. No clear message
Visitors can't tell what you do in five seconds. Fix: a plain headline stating what you offer and for whom.

## 2. No call to action
The site describes the business but never asks the visitor to *do* anything. Fix: one clear action per page.

## 3. Slow load
Heavy images and bloat drive people away. Fix: compress images, use good hosting.

## 4. Not mobile-friendly
Most visitors are on phones, but the site was built for desktop. Fix: mobile-first design.

## 5. Hidden contact details
Customers have to hunt to reach you. Fix: visible phone, WhatsApp and email.

## 6. Weak or no proof
No reviews, no examples, just claims. Fix: add real testimonials and results.

## 7. Outdated content
Old prices, dead links, a stale copyright year. Fix: keep it current.

## 8. Doing everything yourself badly
A DIY site that looks it can cost more in lost trust than it saves. Fix: get the important things done properly.
