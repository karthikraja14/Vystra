Speed is one of the few things that affects both your Google ranking *and* your conversion rate. Here's what actually slows sites down — and how to fix it.

## Why speed matters
Visitors leave sites that take more than a few seconds to load, and Google uses page speed as a ranking signal. On mobile data, a heavy site is even more punishing.

## The biggest culprits

1. **Huge images** — the number one cause. Photos straight from a phone are often 10x larger than needed.
2. **Too many scripts** — every plugin and tracker adds weight.
3. **Cheap/slow hosting** — a bargain server means a slow site.
4. **No caching** — the site rebuilds everything on every visit.
5. **Bloated themes** — heavy templates loading things you never use.

## How to fix it

- **Compress and resize images** before uploading; use modern formats.
- **Remove unused plugins and scripts.**
- **Use good hosting** or a fast static setup.
- **Enable caching and a CDN.**
- **Lazy-load** images below the fold.

## Measure it
Test with a free tool like PageSpeed Insights. Aim for a fast load on mobile, not just desktop.
