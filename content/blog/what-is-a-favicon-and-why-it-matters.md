Look at the top of your browser — each tab has a tiny icon next to the title. That's a **favicon**. It's small, but it does real work for your brand.

## Where favicons show up
- Browser tabs.
- Bookmarks and favourites.
- Phone home screens (when someone saves your site).
- Browser history.

## Why it matters
- **Professionalism.** A blank or default icon looks unfinished. A proper favicon signals a real, cared-for business.
- **Recognition.** With many tabs open, people find yours by its icon.
- **Trust.** Small polish details add up to "this business pays attention."

## What makes a good favicon
- Simple — it's tiny, so detail disappears.
- Usually your logo mark or a single letter.
- Clear at very small sizes.
- Matches your brand colours.

## The takeaway
A favicon is a small detail that quietly separates a professional site from an unfinished one. It takes minutes to add — and every Vystra site ships with a proper favicon so your brand looks complete everywhere it appears.
