A call-to-action (CTA) is the button or line that tells a visitor what to do next — "Get a Quote," "Book a Call," "Buy Now." It's one of the most important few words on your entire website.

## Why CTAs matter so much
People don't act unless you tell them to. A page without a clear CTA leaves visitors thinking "…now what?" — and they leave. A strong CTA turns interest into an enquiry.

## What makes a good CTA

**1. Be specific.**
"Get Your Free Quote" beats "Submit." Say exactly what happens.

**2. Use action words.**
Start with a verb: Get, Book, Start, Download, Call.

**3. Reduce the risk.**
"Free," "No obligation," "2-minute" — lower the perceived cost of clicking.

**4. Make it stand out.**
Your CTA button should be the most visible thing on the page (that's what your accent colour is for).

**5. One main action per page.**
Don't offer five choices. Pick the *one* thing you most want them to do.

## Where to put them
- Top of the page (for ready-to-act visitors).
- After you've explained the benefit.
- At the end of the page.

## The takeaway
A clear, specific, low-risk CTA is the difference between a visitor who leaves and one who contacts you. At Vystra we design every page around one clear action — making it obvious and easy for customers to take the next step.
