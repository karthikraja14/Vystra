People buy from businesses they trust — and they judge trust in seconds, mostly subconsciously. Here's how to make your website earn it.

## Trust signals that work

1. **Real photos** — of your work, team, premises. Stock photos feel hollow.
2. **Genuine reviews** — with names, and ideally results.
3. **Clear contact details** — a real address, phone and email.
4. **Professional design** — clean, consistent, no broken bits.
5. **Fast, secure site** — the padlock (HTTPS) and quick load.
6. **Specific proof** — numbers and case studies beat adjectives.
7. **A clear About page** — who's behind the business.

## Trust killers to avoid

- Spelling and grammar mistakes.
- Broken links, forms that don't work, missing images.
- No contact information (feels like hiding).
- Outdated content — a copyright year from three years ago.
- Aggressive pop-ups the moment you land.

## The psychology
Every small friction — a slow page, a vague claim, a dead link — chips away at confidence. Every proof point rebuilds it. Trust is the sum of these details.
