Bounce rate is the percentage of visitors who land on your site and leave without clicking anything or visiting another page. A high bounce rate often means something's putting people off — so it's worth understanding.

## What counts as a "bounce"
Someone arrives, looks at one page, and leaves without interacting. They didn't click a button, fill a form, or visit another page.

## Is a high bounce rate always bad?
Not necessarily. Context matters:
- A **single-page site** or a blog post that fully answers a question will naturally have a high bounce rate — and that's fine.
- A **homepage or services page** with a high bounce rate is a warning sign — people aren't finding a reason to stay or act.

## Common causes of a high bounce rate
- **Slow loading** — people leave before the page appears.
- **Poor mobile experience** — hard to read or use on a phone.
- **Unclear message** — visitors can't tell what you offer.
- **No clear next step** — nothing tells them what to do.
- **Mismatched expectations** — the page isn't what they searched for.

## How to reduce it
1. **Speed up** your site.
2. Make the **message clear** in the first few seconds.
3. Add an obvious **call to action**.
4. Ensure it works beautifully on **mobile**.
5. Make content **easy to scan**.

## The takeaway
Bounce rate is a clue, not a verdict — read it in context. On key pages, a high bounce rate usually means slow loading, an unclear message, or no clear action. At Vystra we build fast, focused pages that give visitors a reason to stay and act.
