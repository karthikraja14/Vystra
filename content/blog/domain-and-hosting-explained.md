"Domain" and "hosting" get thrown around as if everyone knows what they mean. Here's the plain-English version so you never overpay or get confused again.

## The simple analogy
- **Domain** = your address (yourbusiness.com). It's what people type to find you.
- **Hosting** = the land and building where your website actually lives.

You rent the domain (yearly) and the hosting (monthly or yearly). Both are needed for a live website.

## What a domain costs
A `.com` or `.in` domain is usually a few hundred to ~₹1,000 per year. Premium names cost more. **You should own it in your name** — never let an agency register it under theirs.

## What hosting costs
It varies hugely:
- **Shared hosting** — cheap, fine for small sites.
- **Cloud/managed hosting** — more reliable, costs more.
- **Static hosting** — fast and often very cheap for marketing sites.

## What you actually need
- **A domain in your own name.**
- **Hosting that matches your site** — a small business site doesn't need enterprise hosting.
- **An SSL certificate** (the padlock) — usually free.
